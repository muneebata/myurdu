Rang aur Pasand · Colors & Likes

Say what you love — in full color

Research on vocabulary retention is clear: words stick when they're about YOU. This level gives you colors plus the 'pasand hai' pattern — from here on, you can state a preference about anything you've learned.

The phrases

لالlāl — redAlso means 'ruby' and 'darling' — a very affectionate color.
نیلاnīlā — blue
ہراharā — greenThe color of Pakistan's flag — 'sabz' is its Persian twin.
پیلاpīlā — yellow
کالاkālā — black
سفیدsafed — white
گلابیgulābī — pinkLiterally 'rose-colored' — from gulāb, rose.
رنگrang — colorAs in Holi's 'rang' — and 'rangīn', colorful.
مجھے نیلا رنگ پسند ہےmujhe nīlā rang pasand hai — I like (the color) bluePattern: mujhe ___ pasand hai = I like ___.
مجھے چائے پسند ہےmujhe chāy pasand hai — I like teaLevels 5 + 9, one sentence. It's all one language now.
آپ کو کیا پسند ہے؟āp ko kyā pasand hai? — What do you like?Level 6's kyā earning its keep. Ask everyone.

Good to know

✨ “Mujhe chāy pasand hai” is literally “to-me tea pleasing is” — likes work like Spanish gustar: the thing you love does the verb, you just receive the feeling. Very romantic grammar.
✨ Gulābī (pink) comes from gulāb (rose), which is Persian for 'rose water' — gul (flower) + āb (water). Inside the word for pink there's a whole garden.
✨ Mehndi green, gulābī pink, surkh deep red — wedding season owns half the Urdu color vocabulary.
