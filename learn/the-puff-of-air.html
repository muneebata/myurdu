The Puff of Air · بھ پھ تھ

Aspiration — consonants that ride out on a breath

Some Urdu consonants come with a built-in puff of breath. Hold your palm at your lips: if it feels the wind, you're doing it right.

Practice this lesson free — with audio, quizzes & games →

The phrases

پھلphal — fruitp + puff — NOT an English 'f'!
کھاناkhānā — foodk + puff
ٹھیکṭhīk — fine / okaycurl AND puff — the full combo

Good to know

✨ Hold your palm in front of your mouth. Say “pin” — feel the puff? Now “spin” — no puff. English does both automatically; Urdu makes them separate sounds, written with ھ.
✨ The pair matters: پل pal (a moment) vs پھل phal (fruit). Same word, one puff apart.
✨ Drill: alternate 'spin-pin, spin-pin' with your palm up, then transfer that puff onto k → kh, t → th, ch → chh.
✨ Aspiration is why 'Pakistan' said by a native speaker sounds different from the English version: the P is unaspirated — no puff — while English blasts it.
