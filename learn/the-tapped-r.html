The Tapped R · ر

The flappy R you already say in the middle of 'butter'

Urdu's r is a single quick tap of the tongue — and your mouth already knows it from everyday English. Learn where it lives, then say it in real words.

The phrases

میراmerā — myone light tap — meh-(tap)-aa
چارchār — fourtap the r at the very end
اردوurdū — Urduur-(tap)-doo — say the language's name right!

Good to know

✨ Urdu's r is not the English growl. It's a single quick tap of the tongue-tip against the ridge behind your top teeth.
✨ You already make this sound! Say “butter” or “water” fast, American-style — that flappy 'tt' in the middle IS the Urdu ر. Now put it where an r goes.
✨ Want a full rolling trill? Relax the tongue-tip, hold it loosely at the ridge, and push air steadily so it flutters — like a cat's purr. Practice with 'tr' words: say “butter-up” faster and faster until the tap buzzes.
✨ English speakers make the Urdu tapped r thousands of times a day without knowing it — every fast “butter”, “water”, and “ladder” contains it. The sound isn't new; only its address is.
