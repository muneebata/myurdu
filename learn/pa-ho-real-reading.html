The phrases

دودھ پتی ۸۰ روپےdūdh pattī assī rupaye — milk tea — 80 rupeesthe rich uncle of chai
سموسہ ۴۰ روپےsamosa chālīs rupaye — samosa — 40 rupeesread it, then crave it
پانی ۳۰ روپےpānī tīs rupaye — water — 30 rupeesR7's assembly word, now on a menu
خوش آمدیدkhush āmdīd — Welcomeover every gate and airport arch
آہستہ چلیںāhista chaleṉ — Go slowpainted near every school
یہاں پارکنگ منع ہےyahāṉ pārking manā hai — No parking heremanā = forbidden; pārking = exactly what it sounds like
دھیان سےdhyān se — With care / attention!the aspirated دھ from Sound School
دعوتdāwat — invitation / feastdāl blocks, ain receives — two joins to spot
ولیمہvalīma — the wedding banquetthe groom's-side feast, L18's world in one word
شادی مبارکshādī mubārak — congratulations on the weddingyou can now READ your entrance line from L18

Good to know

✨ Numbers inside Urdu text flip direction: the words flow right-to-left, but ۵۰ reads left-to-right — your eyes learn to zigzag without noticing.
✨ Hand-painted signboards are a living art in Pakistan — bus and truck calligraphers letter freehand in styles no font can match, which is why two 'khatrah' signs never look quite the same.
