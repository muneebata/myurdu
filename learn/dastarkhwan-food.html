Dastarkhwan · Food

A regional eating tour, from nihari dawn to falooda midnight

The dastarkhwan is the cloth spread for a meal, and Pakistan's is enormous. Every province cooks a different country. Arrive hungry; this unit has no other prerequisite.

Good to know

✨ Punjab: nihari (slow-cooked overnight stew, eaten at DAWN, breakfast of emperors), paye, sarson kā sāg with makkī kī roṭī, and lassi in clay tumblers.
✨ Karachi & Sindh: the famous aloo-wali biryani, bun kebab from street carts, Sindhi sāī bhājī, and nimco by the paper bag.
✨ Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: chapli kebab (a spiced patty the size of your face, fried in sight of the Khyber), kabuli pulao, and namkeen gosht, meat, salt, fat, perfection.
✨ Balochistan: sajji, whole lamb salt-roasted on stakes around a fire, and kaak, bread baked hard on hot stones.
✨ Mithai is a love language: gulab jamun, jalebi (fried spirals of syrup), barfi, kheer. Good news travels in a box of mithai, job, engagement, exam result, all of it.
✨ Falooda: rose syrup, vermicelli, basil seeds, ice cream, is dessert, drink, and architecture at once.
