From the Throat · خ غ ق

The deep sounds English doesn't have

خ, غ, and ق are made far back in the mouth, deeper than any English sound. Once your tongue finds the spot, they're surprisingly comfortable.

The phrases

خدا حافظkhudā hāfiz: goodbyeloch-rasp at the start
غزلghazal: ghazal (love poem)gargled g, gh-uh-zul
قلمqalam: pendeep-throat k: q-uh-lum

Good to know

✨ خ (khe): the rasp in Scottish “loch” or German “Bach”. Push air through a nearly-closed throat. You've been saying it in khudā hāfiz all along.
✨ غ (ghain): the same rasp, but voiced: a gentle gargle, like the French r in “Paris”. It starts the word ghazal.
✨ ق (qāf): a k made deeper in the throat, at the uvula. Feel your tongue's back touch further down. In relaxed speech many speakers soften it toward k, so close is fine.
