The Retroflex Curl · ٹ ڈ ڑ

Curl the tongue back — the sounds that make Urdu sound like Urdu

Curl your tongue-tip up and back and touch the roof of your mouth. That one move unlocks ٹ, ڈ, and ڑ — the 'heavy' letters.

The phrases

بیٹاbeṭā — soncurl on the ṭ: bay-(curl)-aa
آٹھāṭh — eightcurl + a puff of air
روٹیroṭī — flatbreadtapped r, then curled ṭ — both drills in one word
لڑکاlaṛkā — boythe ڑ flap: luh-(curl-tap)-kaa

Good to know

✨ These are the sounds that make Urdu sound like Urdu. Curl your tongue-tip up and back to the roof of your mouth, then flick it forward as you release.
✨ English t/d are halfway there already — just exaggerate the curl. Urdu ears hear English 't' as ٹ, which is why 'Tom' gets written ٹام.
✨ ڑ (ṛe) is the boss level: a retroflex flap — curl back, then tap while uncurling. It never starts a word, so you always have a running start.
✨ The retroflex ٹ ڈ ڑ don't exist in Arabic or Persian — the subcontinent added them, marking the letters with a tiny ط on top. When you nail them, you sound South Asian, not Middle Eastern.
